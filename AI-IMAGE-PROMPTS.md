## 🎯 Doporučené nastavení pro OpenArt AI

### Základní parametry:
- **Model:** Stable Diffusion XL nebo podobný
- **Sampler:** DPM++ 2M Karras nebo Euler a
- **Steps:** 30-50
- **CFG Scale:** 7-9
- **Seed:** Random (nebo fixed pro konzistenci postav)

### Negative prompts (co NECHCETE):
```
ugly, distorted, blurry, low quality, bad anatomy, deformed, poorly drawn, modern style, realistic, photographic, dark, scary, horror, adult content, violence
```

### Style modifiers (přidejte ke každému promptu):
```
, high quality, detailed, vibrant colors, professional illustration, clean lines, appealing to children, safe for kids
```
